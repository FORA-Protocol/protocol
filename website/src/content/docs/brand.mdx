## Let your agent do it

The skill is a folder with a `SKILL.md`, the logo files, color and type tokens,
and a naming and claims reference. It works in any agent that reads Agent Skills,
and the `SKILL.md` is plain Markdown that any other agent can follow.

**Claude (web, desktop, mobile).** Download
[`fora-brand.zip`](/brand/fora-brand.zip). In Claude, open **Customize → Skills**,
choose **+ → Create skill → Upload a skill**, upload the zip and switch it on.
Skills need code execution turned on in **Settings → Capabilities**.

**Claude Code.** Unzip it into your personal skills folder,
`~/.claude/skills/fora-brand/`, or into `.claude/skills/fora-brand/` in a repository
to share it with your team. If you already uploaded it to your Claude account and
sign in to Claude Code with that account, it syncs on its own.

**Any other agent.** Give it [`SKILL.md`](/brand/SKILL.md) and
[`messaging.md`](/brand/messaging.md) as instructions and the logo files from the
logo pack.

Then ask for the thing you need:

```text
Make a 6-slide intro to FORA for a publisher audience. Use the fora-brand skill.
```

## How to talk about FORA

Lead with what FORA does for the owner of the content. The mechanism comes
second.

<div class="bk-pitch">
  <p class="bk-pitch-h">Every AI agent is a customer. <em>FORA is your storefront.</em></p>
  <p>People are starting to search, research and buy through AI agents instead
  of websites. FORA lets any agent find your pages or tracks, read your license,
  pay, and get a signed receipt. You decide every term.</p>
</div>

When you need the outcome in one line: FORA makes a website or a music catalog
licensable to AI agents, instantly. Set your terms once, and every page or track
becomes a signed offer any agent can find, buy and verify, with no deal to
negotiate per buyer.

For an engineering audience, start one step lower: FORA is an open transaction
protocol that lets AI agents discover, license, pay for and receive content on
terms the rights holder sets, with every step signed.

**"Instantly" has one meaning.** Once a site or catalog is connected, every page
or track is on sale to every compatible agent at once, on the owner's terms.
It does not mean there is no setup. Don't promise minutes or one click; name the
setup instead:

| Who | The one-time setup | Read more |
|---|---|---|
| A website | Choose your terms on the publisher console, serve one file at `/.well-known/fora.json`, install one package on your CDN. No CMS changes. | [Publisher Onboarding](/getting-started/publisher-onboarding/) |
| A music catalog | Describe tracks, licenses and prices in one feed and push it to an Exchange; set up delivery from your domain and CDN once. No code. | [Live Demo: Music](/getting-started/live-demo-music/#for-catalog-owners) |
| A marketplace | Keep your catalog, search and delivery; publish the catalog as a feed. | [For Content Marketplaces](/getting-started/existing-marketplace/) |

**What the owner gets**, in this order: search, terms an agent can read,
payment, delivery and evidence. Each license becomes a signed offer, each
purchase a signed record anyone can check later.

**Tone.** Plain, specific, a little dry. Show what is signed, by whom, and what
the agent receives, instead of adjectives. No "revolutionary", "seamless",
"unlock" or "game-changing".

## Logo

The mark is a wax seal pressed with the open forum. Before signatures and
cryptography, a wax seal proved who sent a letter and that nobody had opened it
on the way, and any recipient could check it without asking a central office.
That is what FORA's signed offers and acceptances do. The impression is an open
square with no walls, the forum anyone can enter, around one point: the
transaction it seals.

- Use the lockup by default. Use the seal alone where space is square or tiny, or
  where "FORA" is already written nearby.
- **Under 32 px** (the seal's own height; about 8 mm in print) the impression
  can't be read, so use the plain seal: `fora-mark-small.svg`, or the
  `fora-lockup-small-…` files. The favicon is the plain seal.
- Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the seal's height on every side.
- The seal is always FORA Blue, except in the white and black versions. The
  impression is cut through, so the background shows in it.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolor, outline or add effects, wax textures or 3D.
  Don't put a different emblem in the seal, don't retype "fora protocol" in a
  font as a substitute, and don't attach other words to the mark.

## Type

**Instrument Sans** for everything: headlines 700 with −0.03em tracking, UI 600,
body 400–500. **DM Mono** for code, field and RPC names, IDs and small labels.
Both are free on Google Fonts. Headlines and buttons are sentence case.

## Name and claims

- Write **FORA**. Use "the FORA protocol" or "FORA Protocol" when you need the
  noun. The lowercase "fora protocol" exists only inside the logo. Never "Fora" or
  the old name "RAMP".
- FORA stands for Federated Open Resource Access.
- Call it an **open protocol**. No standards body has adopted it, so not "the
  FORA standard".
- FORA carries the rights holder's terms and a signed acceptance. It does not
  grant rights, clear rights or give legal cover; don't say it does.
- AI training is its own license permission and is never implied by another term.
- Demo purchases use test money and grant no real rights.
- Only name customers, partners or numbers that appear on this site.

The full reference, with facts you may state and phrases to avoid, is
[`messaging.md`](/brand/messaging.md).

## Using the name and logo

The Apache 2.0 and CC BY-ND 4.0 licenses cover the code, specification and
documentation, not the FORA name or logo. You may use the name and logo to refer
to FORA: in articles, talks and diagrams, and as "works with FORA" or "built on
FORA" for software that implements the protocol. Don't use them in your own
product or company name, don't alter them, and don't imply that the FORA project
endorses, certifies or partners with you unless it says so in writing.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/FORA-Protocol/protocol/issues).
